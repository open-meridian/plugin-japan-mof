## 0.1.0

On open-meridian 0.36.0, contract 30, on core aae501d's harness. The
product owner's items 128, 130 and 140 (2026-11-19): Tier 1, the JGB
constant-maturity curve as par yields with the Ministry's semiannual
compounding. The dataset key, the record's code and the source `mof` are
proposed here, the product owner's to approve.

- **`mof_curves`** (push and pull): the JGB interest rates on a constant
  maturity basis, 1 to 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 and 40 years, each a `par_yield`
  at its tenor on one `rate_curve` record. Semiannual compounding, the
  Ministry's statement; NL/365, the plugin's. A tenor the Ministry writes as
  `-` is no row.
- **Two files:** the current month, read every hour; the history since
  1974, read only when the lake wants an earlier day, at most once a day.
- **Kept:** the Public Data License (Version 1.0) allows commercial use with
  a source citation; every answer is kept as received.
- **No record minted;** no published time (the Ministry states none on a
  value). No forwards or curve parameters (ruling 131).
- **Terms in words** (`declared.TERMS`), JSDA's prices named.
- **Pages:** the data role's Connection (with Sources) and Datasets (with
  Records).
- **Sample data:** the Ministry's two files as received on 2026-11-19.
