# Terms

A summary, not legal advice: read the Ministry's own words before relying on
them. Terms change; these were read on 19 November 2026.

From the Ministry of Finance's notice on copyright
([mof.go.jp/english/about_mof/notice](https://www.mof.go.jp/english/about_mof/notice/index.html))
and its interest rates' Q&A
([mof.go.jp/english/policy/jgbs/reference/interest_rate/qa.htm](https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/jgbs/reference/interest_rate/qa.htm)):

- **The site's content.** Its copyright belongs to the Ministry unless
  otherwise provided, and the Public Data License (Version 1.0) applies
  unless any rights are indicated.
- **The Public Data License.** Use, commercial use included, with a source
  citation, and a statement where the content is edited; numerical data and
  simple tables are outside its terms. The plugin edits no value; the source
  citation is on the Datasets page.
- **Where the rates come from.** The Ministry calculates them from the Japan
  Securities Dealers Association's Reference Statistical Prices (Yields) for
  OTC Bond Transactions. The Ministry's pages do not state the
  Association's own terms.

The dataset describes these terms in words, shown on the Data sources page,
and declares no licence. The licence a deployment admin sets there is the
one enforced; the plugin says nothing of whether your deployment meets
anyone's terms.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Ministry of Finance.
